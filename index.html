              Departmental QA Best Practices

              Accreditation & Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) Guide

              Essential protocols for Course Review Folders (CRFs) and closing the loop under PEC & Washington Accord standards.

              Course Review Folder (CRF) Mandatory Checklist

              • ✓ Approved Course Syllabus: Explicitly displaying CLO statements, mapped PEC PLOs, and Bloom's cognitive domain.
              • ✓ Assessment Alignment Matrices: Midterm and final exam papers with question-to-CLO mapping table and marks weighting.
              • ✓ Graded Student Samples: Best, average, and worst answer scripts demonstrating transparent rubric scoring.
              • ✓ CLO Attainment Calculation: Direct assessment calculation demonstrating student cohort percentage achieving benchmark (e.g. 50% threshold).
              • ✓ CQI Action Report: Documented instructor reflections addressing deficient outcomes with actionable interventions for the next offering.

              The OBE Continuous Quality Improvement Cycle

              1. Plan (Constructive Alignment) Define measurable CLOs using active verbs. Align delivery methods with intended cognitive depths.
              2. Execute & Audit (Assessment) Verify that exam questions do not suffer from cognitive deflation (e.g., Q3 testing recall for a design CLO).
              3. Evaluate & Close the Loop (CQI) Calculate cohort attainment. If attainment is below 60%, formulate remedial lab sessions or revised assessment strategies.
